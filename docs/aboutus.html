Why the Kiangombe Dispensary Platform Exists

The Kiangombe Health Center Dispensary blends on-site clinicians with a digital-first workflow. By capturing appointments, triage insights, vitals, and prescription decisions in one place, we shorten queues, reduce stock-outs, and keep families updated through SMS.

  • Seamless intake from USSD, walk-ins, and CHV outreach
  • Single longitudinal record for every patient
  • Automated reminders for reviews, labs, and refills
  • Analytics that feed county reporting templates

Four Pillars of Coordinated Care

Smart Scheduling

Visit templates, triage tags, and colored queues keep the waiting bay flowing while flagging emergencies.

Clinical Workspace

SOAP notes, vitals, and lab requests sit beside previous visits so clinicians can act quickly.

Prescription Tracking

QR validated scripts, drug interaction alerts, and stock balances per pharmacy outlet.

Community Follow-up

CHV dashboards map households due for check-ins, vaccinations, and chronic disease reviews.

The Appointment-to-Prescription Journey

01
Patient Intake

Symptoms, vitals, and past meds captured via portal, CHV tablets, or front desk kiosks.

02
Consult & Decide

Clinicians review longitudinal data, issue care plans, and trigger labs or imaging.

03
Prescription Issued

Digital scripts sign with verified PINs, embed QR codes, and sync to pharmacy stock.

04
Fulfil & Remind

Patients receive SMS pickup alerts, while refill reminders keep chronic cases adherent.

Community Pharmacies Onboarded

Each pharmacy partner scans the QR code, confirms dosage instructions, and updates the ledger so clinicians see exactly what was dispensed.
