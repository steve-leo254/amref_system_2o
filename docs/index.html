Appointment Workflow

1. Intake & Triage

Patients fill out symptoms, vitals, and supporting files through the web portal or USSD assistant.

2. Smart Scheduling

Automated rules match visit types with available clinicians, rooms, and telehealth channels.

3. Consultation

Doctors capture notes, order labs, and draft prescriptions while nurses update vitals in real time.

4. Fulfillment & Follow-up

Digital prescriptions route to pharmacies, and follow-up reminders keep chronic patients adherent.

Prescription Tracking That Closes the Loop

Each prescription carries a secure QR code referenced in the Kiangombe ledger. Pharmacies verify authenticity instantly, record dispensed quantities, and trigger patient notifications for pickups and refills.

  • Interaction alerts across recorded allergies and active meds
  • Stock depletion warnings per pharmacy partner
  • Chronic disease refill plans with automated SMS nudges
  • Analytics exported to Kiambu County health dashboards
